Hand-chosen employer logos, which win over the automatic lookup (radar/logos.py, import_manual).
Name each file after the employer's slug as the site makes it (lower case, runs of other characters as one
dash: "Royal Schiphol Group" -> royal-schiphol-group.png). PNG, JPEG, WebP, SVG or ICO; square and at least
128 px is best. A logo drawn for a dark background (white lettering) is named <slug>.dark.svg (or
.png) and shown on a dark plate. The worker loads them on its daily logo run, or now with `radar logos`.
